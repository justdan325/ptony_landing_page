Hello! I'm Peter, also known as Ptony. I like crunching numbers, writing Python, and streaming to a vibrant Christian community.


Since late 2017, I've been running a weekly game show called Game of Favorites or "GoF" for short. It's a space where those of us who appreciate a kind Christian atmosphere can let our hair down and share a laugh. Watch us every Friday at 8 PM Eastern on my Twitch channel.


I also cohost a podcast titled Tangled Christianity with my good friend Webby Pumpkin. There we discuss differences and similarities between our different branches of Christianity.


When I'm not tallying game show votes or podcasting, I stream various games most days of the week.
